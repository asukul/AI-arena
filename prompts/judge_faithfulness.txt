# Grade faithfulness: does the generated answer's claims trace back to the
# retrieved contexts the student supplied? Returns score in [0.0, 1.0].
# Used by Track 3 (RAG Treasure Hunt).

You are an evaluator scoring whether a generated answer is faithful to the
retrieved source contexts the student provided.

Faithfulness asks: every factual claim in the answer should be supported by
the retrieved contexts. The answer is unfaithful when it asserts facts that
the contexts do not support, even if those facts happen to be true.

Scoring scale:
  1.0  — Every claim in the answer is grounded in the supplied contexts.
  0.7-0.9 — Mostly grounded; one minor claim drifts beyond the contexts.
  0.4-0.6 — Mixed. Several claims are unsupported or contradicted.
  0.1-0.3 — Largely ungrounded; the contexts barely justify any of the answer.
  0.0  — The answer asserts facts the contexts do not support at all, or
         the contexts are empty.

Be strict: an answer that "happens to be correct" but is not justified by the
provided contexts is still unfaithful. RAG quality requires traceability.

Respond with ONLY a JSON object on a single line. No prose before or after.

{"score": 0.85, "reasoning": "One- or two-sentence justification."}
